💡 Budget Travel Tips

Save more, explore more across India

🚂 Transport Tips

  • Book train tickets 60-120 days in advance on IRCTC for best availability and prices
  • Use Sleeper Class (SL) for short journeys and 3AC for overnight trips — much cheaper than flights
  • Red Bus and AbhiBus offer discounted sleeper buses for intercity travel
  • Within cities, use local buses and metro instead of autos/cabs to save ₹100-200/day
  • Rent a bicycle or scooter in tourist towns like Hampi and Pushkar for ₹100-200/day

🏨 Accommodation Tips

  • Stay in hostels — dorm beds start at ₹200-400/night in most cities
  • Dharamshalas near temples offer free or very cheap stays (₹50-100) in pilgrimage towns
  • Book OYO or Treebo rooms 3-7 days in advance for 30-50% discounts
  • Couchsurfing is free and you get local insights — great for solo travelers
  • Airbnb homestays are often cheaper than hotels and include kitchen access

🍛 Food Tips

  • Eat at dhabas and local thali restaurants — ₹60-120 for a complete meal
  • Street food is your best friend — samosas, vada pav, chai cost ₹10-30 each
  • Avoid restaurants near tourist spots — they charge 3-5x more
  • Carry a water bottle and refill at filtered water stations to avoid buying bottles
  • Cook your own breakfast in hostels with kitchen facilities to save ₹100-150/day

🎭 Activities Tips

  • Most temples, ghats, beaches and public parks are free to enter
  • Get an ASI annual pass for ₹1500 — gives free entry to 100+ monuments including Taj Mahal
  • Student ID cards give 50-75% discount at most government monuments
  • Sunrise visits to popular spots are free and less crowded than paid evening events
  • Join free walking tours in cities like Delhi, Mumbai and Jaipur run by volunteers

📱 Apps Every Budget Traveler Needs

  • IRCTC Rail Connect — book train tickets easily
  • RedBus — compare and book bus tickets
  • OYO / MakeMyTrip — find budget hotels
  • Google Maps — offline maps, no data needed
  • UPI / Google Pay — cashless payments everywhere
